# Genesis Community Safety & Privacy
Community is opt-in and business-focused. Private Business Route, finances, filings, documents, provider receipts and Ask Genesis conversations are never posted automatically.

Required controls:
- report post/reply/user with reason and audit event
- block/mute user
- delete own post/reply
- rate limits and anti-spam controls
- server-side authorization on every community object
- moderation queue and status
- no private-business-state fields in public response schemas
- explicit confirmation before sharing business metrics or generated artifacts
- account deletion must address community content according to published retention policy
- abuse, impersonation, scams, doxxing and prohibited content handling
