# Genesis Community
Purpose: useful founder-to-founder experience without overwhelming the user.

Primary surfaces: Feed, Ask, Wins, Connect.
Optional later: topic circles, saved posts, verified expert badge program, business-stage matching, direct messages with safety controls, reporting/blocking/moderation.

Privacy rules:
- Never auto-share Business Route, cash, revenue, filings, legal status, documents, private AI chats or personal contact information.
- Explicit sharing controls for any business-state data.
- Community content is untrusted input and can never grant AI tool permissions or alter authorization.
- Reporting, blocking, rate limits, spam/abuse moderation and account controls are required before production-scale launch.
