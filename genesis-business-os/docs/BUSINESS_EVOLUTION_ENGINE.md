# GENESIS BUSINESS OS — Business Evolution Engine

## Product rule
Power underneath. Simplicity on top.

The Evolution Engine does not add another navigation area. It lives behind Home and My Business and extends the existing Genesis loop:

Founder Reality → Destination → Route → Next Turn → Execute/Prepare/Guide → Proof → Outcome → Business DNA → Possible Futures → Recommended Evolution → Reroute.

## Consumer experience
The user sees one recommended evolution, a short reason, the allowed cash envelope, and one next action. “See my best future” can reveal the compared futures without forcing the user to manage simulations.

## Deterministic inputs
- founder cash and protected reserve
- destination monthly income
- current monthly revenue
- repeat-customer share
- gross margin
- capacity used

## Current possible futures
- Validate demand deeper
- Strengthen repeat customers
- Test pricing
- Increase customer acquisition
- Increase capacity

The engine scores the futures with deterministic rules and recommends the highest-scoring current path. It does not guarantee revenue or fabricate proof.

## Business DNA
The engine derives simple operating signals from real inputs:
- repeat-customer signal
- margin signal
- capacity signal
- protected cash rule

Future versions should replace manually supplied metrics with verified actuals from connected payments, accounting, CRM, execution receipts, and outcome capture wherever authorized.

## Safety / truthfulness
Possible futures are decision support, not guarantees. Spending remains bounded by the Resource Envelope. High-consequence execution continues through the existing authorization gateway and EXECUTE / PREPARE / GUIDE model.
