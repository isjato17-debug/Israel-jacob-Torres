====================================================
1. PERSONAL AI BUSINESS CONSULTANT
====================================================

Every customer receives a persistent,
personalized AI business consultant.

B.I.G. must:

- Understand the customer's business goals.
- Evaluate business ideas.
- Develop business strategies.
- Generate and compare business plans.
- Analyze startup costs.
- Identify financial risks.
- Recommend practical improvements.
- Explain business decisions.
- Help customers solve business problems.
- Guide customers through business growth.
- Remember authorized business context.
- Adapt recommendations as circumstances change.

The consultant must connect directly to
the customer's actual Business Projects,
financial records, tasks, and documents.

Never invent financial results or
claim actions occurred when they did not.

3. CONSULTING PLUS EXECUTION
====================================================

B.I.G. must not stop after giving advice.

When the customer approves a recommended
business action, B.I.G. should create
and execute the supported workflow.

EXAMPLE:

CUSTOMER:
"I want to start a cleaning business."

B.I.G.:

1. Evaluates the idea.
2. Generates three business plans.
3. Helps select the best-fit approach.
4. Identifies applicable requirements.
5. Creates the Business Project.
6. Collects necessary information.
7. Prepares supported paperwork.
8. Presents documents for review.
9. Obtains required signatures.
10. Coordinates authorized submissions.
11. Tracks verified results.
12. Helps establish operations.
13. Tracks income and expenses.
14. Monitors compliance obligations.
15. Recommends growth opportunities.

The customer should not need to
manage disconnected systems.
