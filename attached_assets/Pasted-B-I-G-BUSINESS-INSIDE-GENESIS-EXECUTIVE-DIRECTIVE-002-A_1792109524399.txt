B.I.G. — BUSINESS INSIDE GENESIS
EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVE 002-A
MANDATORY ENTREPRENEUR FUNDING & IMMIGRATION SERVICES

This directive supplements the active Phase 2 order.
Do not restart, replace, or interrupt completed work.

OWNER REQUIREMENT:

B.I.G. must provide integrated, execution-oriented
services for immigrant entrepreneurs, visa holders,
business grants, loans, and other legitimate
business funding opportunities.

These are mandatory application functions,
not optional features or informational pages.

CORE CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE:

A customer enters a business idea once.

Using authorized Business Passport information,
B.I.G. must:

1. Analyze business and financing needs.
2. Identify relevant legal and eligibility requirements.
3. Discover legitimate funding opportunities.
4. Match opportunities to published requirements.
5. Explain eligibility uncertainties and restrictions.
6. Compare financing terms and affordability.
7. Identify missing application information.
8. Prepare supported application forms and documents.
9. Request customer review and authorization.
10. Track applications, deadlines, and outcomes.
11. Resume work without duplicate entry or actions.

IMMIGRATION REQUIREMENTS:

Support entrepreneurs across citizenship and
immigration circumstances, subject to applicable law.

Distinguish business ownership, work authorization,
licensing, and funding eligibility.

Do not assume that a visa permits employment or
guarantees eligibility for loans or grants.

Use authoritative guidance and escalate questions
requiring individualized immigration-law advice
to qualified professionals.

FUNDING REQUIREMENTS:

Support verified discovery and matching for:

- Federal, state, and local grant programs.
- Relevant private and nonprofit grants.
- SBA-supported financing and microloans.
- Community development lenders.
- Credit unions and legitimate business lenders.
- Other verified entrepreneurship programs.

Each opportunity must include its source,
eligibility requirements, verification date,
application requirements, and current status.

Never invent opportunities or guarantee funding.

APPLICATION EXECUTION:

Integrate these workflows with:

- Business Passport
- AutoForm
- Master Orchestrator
- Existing document preparation
- Customer approvals
- Mission Control progress tracking

The system must perform supported preparation
tasks automatically.

AI may recommend actions, but deterministic
systems must enforce eligibility rules,
financial calculations, permissions,
dependencies, and authorization boundaries.

Do not submit real applications, perform credit
checks, or transmit sensitive documents without
explicit authorization and approved integrations.

IMPLEMENTATION ORDER:

A. Inspect and reuse existing functionality.
B. Establish structured funding records.
C. Build verified eligibility-matching logic.
D. Connect funding discovery and preparation.
E. Integrate tasks with Master Orchestrator.
F. Expose a usable customer workflow.
G. Test security, accuracy, and persistence.

During Phase 2, implement the maximum safe,
testable functionality within existing resources.

If unavailable external integrations prevent
completion, build the internal preparation and
tracking functions, document the blocker, and
preserve the remaining work as mandatory scope.

Do not pretend unavailable services are active.

ACCEPTANCE TESTS:

Demonstrate:

- Immigrant and visa-holder customer journeys.
- Grant and loan opportunity matching.
- Ineligible and uncertain applicant handling.
- Accurate loan affordability calculations.
- Application draft generation.
- Exact-revision customer approval.
- Sensitive-data protection.
- Cross-customer isolation.
- Resume after logout.
- Duplicate prevention.
- No unauthorized submissions.
- No paid service activation.
- Preservation of existing Phase 1 and 2 tests.

EXECUTIVE REPORT:

For each capability, report:

IMPLEMENTED AND TESTED
PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED
BLOCKED BY EXTERNAL DEPENDENCY
NOT IMPLEMENTED

Provide actual test evidence, remaining blockers,
and the next required development steps.

PROTECTED RESTRICTIONS:

No paid AI activation.
No customer billing.
No real applications or financial commitments.
No deployment.
No changes to protected main.
No Phase 3 authorization.

Continue the existing Phase 2 development.
Do not restart the project.

END DIRECTIVE 002-A.
