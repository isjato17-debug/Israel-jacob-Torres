B.I.G. should provide hands-on AI assistance that uses saved customer context,
asks only for missing information, and carries out permitted work. Static
recommendations and more checklists alone do not meet the user's goal.
The user clarified that a checklist is wanted, with B.I.G. doing the work for
the customer rather than leaving every item for the customer to complete.

**Why:** The user corrected the Phase 1 direction: “No I want hands on ai assistance
so the it can get the job done with out the procrastination”.

**How to apply:** The Phase 1 execution directive explicitly prohibits paid AI
activation while requiring every supported, authorized task to be performed.
Use existing internal tools for this phase rather than treating live AI approval
as a prerequisite for all hands-on execution. Keep deterministic preparation
clearly distinguished from live AI. Real filings, signatures, spending and
publishing retain their separate approval gates. Later explicit executive orders
authorize Phase 2; they do not authorize Phase 3.

Immigrant/visa-holder entrepreneurship and funding discovery, matching, private
application preparation, approvals and tracking are mandatory product functions,
not optional informational pages. Unavailable integrations do not remove that scope.

**Why:** The owner explicitly supplemented Phase 2 with Directive 002-A.

**How to apply:** Reuse consented Passport data and deterministic authorization/
financial rules; never equate ownership with permission to work or funding
eligibility. Report unsupported live discovery/forms/submission honestly and
retain them as mandatory follow-on work, without activating paid services.
