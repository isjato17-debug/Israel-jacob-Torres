name: Genesis OS 2.0 scope
description: Product vision and preservation/authorization boundaries for reconstruction.
---

The user authorizes transformation of the existing Genesis OS into a premium,
mobile-first, AI-powered business ecosystem centered on START MY BUSINESS,
RUN MY BUSINESS and GROW MY BUSINESS. The goal is one account, dashboard,
document vault and unified service management experience.

Preserve existing working functionality, source history, configuration and customer
data. Before reconstruction, create a recoverable backup and inspect the architecture.
Use a separate development branch or environment; do not replace the working app or
create another project/repository.

Do not publish, start paid services, make purchases, submit government filings or
perform destructive operations without explicit approval. Do not portray unverified
connections as operational. Regulated services require appropriate professional oversight.

**Why:** The user's Genesis OS 2.0 master directive explicitly expands the earlier
infrastructure-only scope while retaining these preservation and approval requirements.

**How to apply:** Treat the previous no-redesign constraint as limited to the original
production-readiness work, not a prohibition on authorized isolated reconstruction.
Continue safe independent development while stating incomplete capabilities honestly.

The reconstruction must prioritize a complete, reliable initial customer journey
before advanced capabilities: one question at a time, automatically saved progress,
authorized business-information reuse and communication adapted to customer preferences.
The latest directive names ten core systems: Mission Control, Business Brain,
Autopilot, Adaptive Intelligence, Business Passport, First Customer Engine, Financial
Command, Business Health Monitor, Enterprise Architect and the optional Business Network.
It additionally requires Genesis Infinite Growth for owner-controlled acquisition,
retention, campaigns, budgets, approvals, analytics and lawful revenue growth.

**Why:** The user's reconstruction-and-innovation directive explicitly defines these
systems and sequencing. It also requires accessibility/usability testing with real users.

**How to apply:** Do not substitute a service catalog for a completed supported
customer journey, assume education/experience, or describe automated browser testing
as a real-user usability study. Distinguish prepared, submitted, pending, approved and
completed using genuine status and evidence; retain approval gates for consequential work.

Target WCAG 2.2 AA; support English and Spanish initially with extensible translation
architecture. Infinite Growth must not use fake engagement, misleading testimonials,
spam, unauthorized publication or guaranteed/permanent-growth claims. Customer success
stories require consent; participation in the Business Network is optional.

**Why:** The user's ultimate reconstruction directive explicitly adds these systems,
accessibility target and growth safeguards while retaining preservation/approval rules.

**How to apply:** Build modular domains rather than an unverified service catalog.
Do not infer platform-operator privileges from customer business-owner status.
Internal content review never by itself authorizes paid spend or external publication.

The complete directive adds Genesis OnePay, requiring itemized pricing, separate
government fees, customer charge approval, authorized provider onboarding/payouts,
subscriptions, refunds/disputes and lawful commissions. Preserve existing Square while
evaluating Stripe Connect; never independently hold/transmit customer funds or store
raw card data. Purchased services must accurately distinguish PAID, ASSIGNED,
IN PROGRESS, SUBMITTED and COMPLETED, with failure/refund/action states as necessary.

**Why:** The user supplied a complete seven-phase reconstruction directive after the
earlier truncated upload. It prioritizes Phase 2 customer experience/AI/Passport before
formation, OnePay/marketplace/admin, broader management and advanced growth automation.

**How to apply:** Follow the complete phase order, preserve earlier safe work, and
do not let a verified payment automatically mark a purchased service completed.

The current master directive authorizes M0 and the first working vertical slice
of M1 only: inspect/preserve existing capabilities, repair missing consultation
privacy controls, and deliver a saved idea → three-plan comparison → selected
Business Project journey. M2 through M10 remain architecture, not authorized
implementation or live activation. Keep the full lifelong business vision; do not
substitute a generic roadmap for verified working customer flows.

**Why:** The user completed the truncated master directive with explicit bounded
execution and the acceptance scenario “I want to start a cleaning business in
New York with $3,000.”

**How to apply:** Preserve advanced Genesis features, Partner Network, languages
and existing records. Use isolated QA data; commit verified local work on the
existing development branch. Do not change Git credentials, repeatedly retry
failed pushes, create replacement branches, force-push, merge protected main,
deploy, publish or activate paid/external actions. Leave the two directive
uploads untouched unless removal is separately authorized. If the milestone
exceeds a safe session, deliver a coherent tested portion and disclose the rest.
