The user authorizes transformation of the existing Genesis OS into a premium,
mobile-first, AI-powered business ecosystem centered on START MY BUSINESS,
RUN MY BUSINESS and GROW MY BUSINESS. The goal is one account, dashboard,
document vault and unified service management experience.

Preserve existing working functionality, source history, configuration and customer
data. Before reconstruction, create a recoverable backup and inspect the architecture.
Use a separate development branch or environment; do not replace the working app or
create another project/repository.

Do not publish, start paid services, make purchases, submit government filings or
perform destructive operations without explicit approval. Do not portray unverified
connections as operational. Regulated services require appropriate professional oversight.

**Why:** The user's Genesis OS 2.0 master directive explicitly expands the earlier
infrastructure-only scope while retaining these preservation and approval requirements.

**How to apply:** Treat the previous no-redesign constraint as limited to the original
production-readiness work, not a prohibition on authorized isolated reconstruction.
Continue safe independent development while stating incomplete capabilities honestly.
